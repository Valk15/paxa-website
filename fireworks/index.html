In one of the largest owner surveys on firework fear, covering 1,225 dogs, herbal remedies, supplements and pheromone products scored in the range you would expect from a placebo. The highest-ranked approach — teaching the dog that a bang predicts food or play — had 71% reported success.

PAXA Fireworks turns that research into a plan you can use on the night, and a programme that changes how your dog responds next season.
